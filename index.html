Frequently asked questions

Something else on your mind? Start a free journal and look around. It takes a minute.

Open Keel
Do I need to connect my broker?

No. You log trades yourself in about twenty seconds each, with a screenshot if you want one. Writing the trade down is a big part of what makes journaling work.

Is my trading data private?

Yes. Your trades and screenshots are stored in your own account, and database rules stop anyone else from reading them. Screenshots are served through short-lived private links.

Does it work for prop-firm evaluations?

Yes. The prop buffer calculator shows how much room you have before your drawdown limit, and you can keep evaluation and live accounts apart.

Which markets can I journal?

Anything you trade: index futures, forex, metals, energies, stocks and crypto. You choose the symbol, session and setup.

Will Keel tell me what to trade?

No. Keel is a journal and analytics tool. It shows you patterns in your own trading. It does not give signals or financial advice.
